# Scoring Rubric

Every Executive Intake item is scored on six dimensions, 1–5, using the anchors below. Anchors exist so two people scoring the same ask land within a point of each other — an unanchored 1–5 is just a mood.

**Rule: score against evidence in the entry, not enthusiasm in the room.** If the entry doesn't contain the evidence for a score, the score isn't defensible — go find it in the research pass, or score lower.

---

## Revenue ROI — weight 0.30

Expected commercial return.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | No revenue line of sight. Internal curiosity. |
| 2 | Indirect support only — better collateral, easier future pitch. |
| 3 | Strengthens an existing pursuit or renewal already in motion. |
| 4 | Named opportunity with a plausible path to close. |
| 5 | Directly unblocks a named deal or renewal with revenue attached. |

*Example — 5:* "Client won't sign without an eval showing our stack beats their incumbent."

---

## Strategic Alignment — weight 0.20

Fit with the [[S0 Charter]] mandate and named bets.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Outside the charter. Would need a slice waiver to touch. |
| 2 | Tangential — fits only by stretch. |
| 3 | Fits a charter slice cleanly. |
| 4 | Advances a named frontier workstream ([[Frontier Radar]], [[Benchmark Discipline]], [[Architecture Hub]], [[Field Response Desk]]). |
| 5 | Advances a named strategic bet **and** leaves a reusable pattern behind. |

*Example — 5:* A build that ships a reference architecture S8 squads then consume.

---

## Client Impact / Visibility — weight 0.20

Who sees the consequence if this goes well — or badly.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Internal to the squad. Invisible elsewhere. |
| 2 | Visible inside Searce. |
| 3 | Visible to one client team. |
| 4 | Touches a live client commitment or an exec-level audience. |
| 5 | Touches a must-win moment — QBR, active pursuit, escalation — with reputational stake. |

---

## Urgency — weight 0.15

Time-sensitivity, evidenced by a date. **No external date → maximum 2.** This is the single most-inflated dimension; the anchor is deliberately strict.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | No date. Can wait a quarter without cost. |
| 2 | Wanted soon, no fixed date. |
| 3 | Tied to a monthly cadence or month-end. |
| 4 | Named external date ≤2 weeks out. |
| 5 | Named external date ≤48h, or blocking someone else's committed deadline. |

*Example — 4 not 5:* "Before their QBR" with the QBR ten days out.

---

## Culture — weight 0.15

Does doing this leave the squad better than it found it.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pure grind. Teaches nothing, reusable by no one. |
| 2 | Minor skill gain for one person. |
| 3 | Produces a documented pattern. |
| 4 | Builds capability in ≥2 people, or yields a teach-in. |
| 5 | Creates a reusable asset the squad — or S8 squads — pull from repeatedly. |

---

## Complexity — weight 0.25, **subtracted**

Cost, difficulty, and uncertainty. Higher complexity pulls the Overall Score **down**: a valuable ask that is disproportionately hard ranks below an equally valuable one that is cheap.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hours. Known path, existing assets. |
| 2 | ~2 days. Known path. |
| 3 | ~1 week. Some unknowns. |
| 4 | Multi-week. Real unknowns or external dependencies. |
| 5 | Unbounded or research-grade. Needs new capability or a third-party dependency. |

*Score the honest number.* Deflating complexity to make a favoured ask rank higher is the one move that breaks the instrument.

---

## Sequencing slots (not scored — assigned)

Rank tells you the order. The slot tells you what's actually moving this week.

| Slot | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Now** | In flight this week. Ranked high **and** the people are free. |
| **Next** | Ranked, waiting on capacity. The default resting state. |
| **Blocked** | Waiting on an external dependency, a decision, or an approval. |
| **Done** | Delivered. Keeps its score as a record of what shipped and what it cost; no longer competes for capacity. Logged with [[Executive Intake Entry (Shipped)]]. |

Re-checked at the weekly sync. **Re-slotting never edits a score** — an ask's merit doesn't change because we got busy.

---

## Reading the Overall Score

Range: **−0.25 to 4.75**. Rough bands:

| Band | Reading |
|---|---|
| ≥ 3.5 | Strong. Sequence it now. |
| 2.5 – 3.5 | Solid. Normal queue. |
| 1.5 – 2.5 | Weak, or valuable-but-expensive. Ask what would make it cheaper. |
| < 1.5 | Costs more than it returns as scoped. Renegotiate scope, don't just queue it. |

A low score is not a refusal. It's the start of a better conversation: *"this is a 1.2 as scoped — here's the version that scores a 3."*
