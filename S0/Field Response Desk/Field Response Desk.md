# Field Response Desk (hot desk / ICU intake)

Part of the 40% frontier slice ([[S0 Charter]] §4.4) *and* the intake point for the separate 20% hot-desk/ICU slice ([[S0 Charter]] §4, "the other 60%"). Two different things share this note — keep them distinct:

## 1. Field response desk (frontier, 40%)
Any solver asks "client is asking about X — what's our answer?" → eval-grounded position within **one business day**, grounded in [[Benchmark Discipline]] results. Every answer that draws on new eval work gets written up as a reusable pattern.

## 2. Hot desk / ICU (urgent, 20%)
Must-win demos, escalations, "the client asks about model X tomorrow." One intake channel; squad lead triages daily; outcome-moving items qualify.

> **Gate: ICU / immediate-build requests require CEO approval before work starts.** This is current squad practice, not stated in the charter PDF — track it here until it's folded into a charter revision.

Cap holds: 20% means 20%. Every hot-desk solve gets written up as a reusable pattern — that's the qualification-gate requirement ([[S0 Charter]] §2).
