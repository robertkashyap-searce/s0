# S0 Squad Charter

> Transcribed from the source PDF so the numbers are queryable/linkable. PDF stays the artifact of record; this is the working copy.

## 1 · What S0 is

**S0** (*ess-zero*) — the zero-indexed squad in the Solver family: the room where patterns are born before S8 squads run them everywhere. Small, capped crew of Searce's sharpest builders. **Every seat renews every quarter.**

### The quarterly time-contract

| Time | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **40%** | **The frontier.** Research, builds, white papers, testing every major model against real client-shaped workloads, shipping reference implementations. The reason S0 exists. |
| **20%** | **Client work.** Real, billable delivery. Research that never meets a client is a hobby. |
| **20%** | **Internal builds.** futurify portal, agent coworkers, the platforms Searce itself runs on. We drink our own champagne. |
| **20%** | **The hot desk.** Must-win demos, escalations, "the client asks about model X *tomorrow*." |

### Three guardrails, non-decorative
- **The 40% is sacred.** Urgent work borrows from the other slices during a crunch; the lab stays a lab.
- **The contract balances quarterly.** Three straight weeks on a must-win demo is fine if the quarter squares up. Measure the integral, not the derivative.
- **The client 20% is real engagements** — agreed with solid-line managers at every quarterly reset.

**Where it lives:** S0 is a Searce-wide institution. Flagship public artifact — the eval-backed **Frontier Radar** — publishes under futurify OS. The neutral benchmark harness stays **internal, always**. Reports to the Applied and Agentic AI Practice Head once seated; interim sponsor is the Field CTO function. *(Open flag: confirm interim sponsor.)*

## 2 · The quarterly bar — three gates

Every quarter, every seat, same three questions:

1. **Did you ship?** ≥1 substantial frontier artifact (white paper, reference architecture, benchmark sweep, deployed build, or Day-Zero Release Note) + every hot-desk solve written up as a reusable pattern.
2. **Did someone use it?** Adoption is data — a squad adopted your pattern, a pursuit cited your eval, your portal feature went live.
3. **Did your day job stay sharp?** Client slice delivered in good standing, frontier time actually landed ≥35%.

Clear all three → seat renews automatically. Clear two → one focus quarter with a written plan back to three. Members who step back do it with honor — alumni form S0's reviewer bench and first-call network. New seats open when one frees up or the sponsor expands the cap.

## 3 · Selection criteria (score 1–5, weights in brackets)

1. **Learning velocity (25%)** — frontier-chasing before anyone asked; learn Monday, ship Friday.
2. **Eval rigor (25%)** — tests failure modes as eagerly as happy paths. Single strongest predictor of S0 value.
3. **Translation instinct (20%)** — the paragraph passes the CEO test, in both directions.
4. **Builder evidence (15%)** — linked work runs; judged on what it proves.
5. **Delivery credibility (10%)** — solid standing on client work + range to parachute into an unfamiliar account. Solid-line manager sign-off before joining.
6. **Teaching reflex (5%)** — has made others better: docs, teach-ins, mentoring.

Panel: Field CTO/senior architect + one rotating Practice lead + one sitting S0 member (once squad exists). Decision within a week.

## 4 · Charter

**Mission:** Searce is always ready. Every frontier shift is measured, translated, and codified into how we build — ahead of the client's question.

### The 40% — four frontier workstreams

1. **Frontier Radar.** Within 48h of any major release: a Day-Zero Release Note — what changed, what it shifts in our reference architectures, what it makes newly possible for clients, eval-backed verdict on real client-shaped workloads. Opinions expire; evals don't.
2. **Benchmark discipline.** Private, model-agnostic eval harness built from anonymized patterns of real engagements. Every model, every quarter, same tasks. **Internal instrument** — publicly, build on every major platform, deep on ours.
3. **Architecture hub.** The paved roads: reference architectures, approved stacks, golden patterns for agentic systems — consumed by S8 squads. Every engagement retools the line; S0 writes the retooling down.
4. **Field response desk.** Any solver asks "client is asking about X — what's our answer?" → eval-grounded position within one business day.

### The other 60%, concrete
- **Client 20%** — one live engagement lane per member per quarter.
- **Internal 20%** — backlog owned by squad lead + futurify GM (once seated): portal capabilities, Ask futurify grounding, agent-coworker patterns, eval tooling.
- **Urgent 20% (hot desk)** — one intake channel, squad lead triages daily, outcome-moving items qualify, every solve leaves a written pattern behind.

### Rhythm
- **Weekly (60 min):** squad sync — what moved, radar updates, hot-desk triage.
- **≤48h per major release:** Day-Zero Release Note → all-solvers channel + futurify Radar.
- **Monthly:** ≥1 reference architecture · ≥1 white paper or build · one teach-in open to all of Searce.
- **Quarterly:** full benchmark sweep · State of the Frontier readout to leadership · pattern handoff to agent-coworker pipeline · time-contract reconciliation · qualification review (three gates, every seat).

### How S0 stays sharp
- **Authority through usefulness** — squads taking a different route write a "why we left the road" note (fuel for the next radar).
- **The hot-desk cap holds.** 20% means 20%.
- **The bar applies to everyone, always** — including the people who built the squad.
- **Delivery is the destination** — knowledge flows outward into squads, patterns, agents. People rotate; agents remain.

## 5 · Success criteria

- **Speed** — 100% of major releases → Day-Zero note ≤48h · desk median answer ≤1 business day · hot-desk spin-up ≤48h from triage.
- **Adoption** — ≥70% of new AI engagements start from an S0 reference architecture within two quarters · every S8 squad consumes ≥1 S0 pattern/quarter · road-deviation notes rich and instructive.
- **Commercial pull** — S0 artifacts cited in proposals (tracked via portal collateral usage) · hot-desk pursuit contributions tracked by win rate · ≥[n] deals/quarter where a radar entry or eval was a client-named reason to believe. *(Open flag: set n after one baseline quarter.)*
- **Output cadence** — per quarter: ≥3 white papers/public-grade builds · ≥3 reference architectures · 1 full benchmark sweep · 100% of hot-desk solves codified.
- **Compounding** — patterns handed to agent-coworker pipeline per quarter · rising share of desk questions answered by existing artifacts.
- **Contract integrity** — frontier time ≥35% actual per member; two consecutive squad-level quarters below → escalate to sponsor.
- **Bar health** — track both healthy turnover (bar is real) and alumni thriving back in BUs (flow works).
- **People** — applications/open seat growing · retention above company average · ≥2 members/year stepping into architect/principal-track elevation (taking on 20–30% of next-level responsibility while performing the current role — the promotion doctrine, lived).
- **Credibility** — squad-lead NPS on S0 usefulness ≥8/10 · neutral benchmark data stays fully internal, every quarter.

**Honesty clause:** S0 earns its existence through adoption. If adoption stays under 40% for three quarters with desk volume falling, restructure the squad around what the field actually pulls. Instruments get recalibrated; so do squads.
